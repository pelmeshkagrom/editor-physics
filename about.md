# <c-34e89e>Editor Physics</c>
Bring real-time <c-34e89e>physics simulation</c> right into the Geometry Dash level editor!

## Usage
1. Select one or multiple objects you want to <c-387fec>apply physics</c> to
2. Click the <c-387fec>toggle physics button</c> in the top menu or press your <c-387fec>customizable keybind</c>
3. The simulation <c-387fec>will start</c>! It will <c-387fec>stop automatically</c> when objects settle, or you can <c-387fec>stop it manually</c> by pressing the button again

## Supported objects
- Solids (Standard blocks)
- Slopes
- Spikes
- Circular objects

## Configuration
- World <c-387fec>gravity strength</c> and <c-387fec>physics refresh rate</c>
- Dynamic objects' <c-387fec>physics parameters</c> (Friction, Bounciness, Damping)
- Toggle button visibility and its position settings
- Customizable keybind for toggling the simulation
